    How it works

    CaptionCraft is a library of hand-written caption templates, organised by category and finished with a tone you choose. Your keyword is dropped into each template so the caption reads like it was written for your photo, not a generic post. Every caption comes with a matching hashtag set from a curated bank for that niche.

    There is no account, no server and no upload: everything happens in your browser, and your saved captions and history never leave this device. Character counts are shown per caption, with a warning when a caption runs past the 280-character limit on X.

    Honest limits: the captions are templates, not a writer. They are a starting point — edit the wording to sound like you before you post, and check that any hashtag still fits your audience.
